# Lab: Exploring the SatNOGS Network and Satellite Telemetry

# Overview

In this lab, you will explore the SatNOGS network, one of the most valuable open-source resources available for satellite research and analysis. SatNOGS is a global community of universities, researchers, amateur radio operators, and satellite enthusiasts who operate ground stations around the world. These ground stations collect telemetry and communications from satellites currently in orbit and make that information available to the broader community.

One of the greatest strengths of the SatNOGS project is that it allows researchers to leverage observations and data that may have already been collected by others. When investigating an unfamiliar satellite, you may discover that other members of the community have already identified it, captured transmissions, or collected telemetry data that can accelerate your research.

# Objectives

By completing this lab, you will:

• Explore the global SatNOGS ground station network.
• Understand how distributed ground stations collect satellite telemetry.
• Identify the geographic distribution of SatNOGS stations worldwide.
• Examine telemetry data collected from satellites.
• Explore Grafana dashboards used to visualize satellite telemetry.
• Understand the types of satellite data available through the SatNOGS ecosystem.
• Learn how community-collected telemetry can support satellite research and analysis.
